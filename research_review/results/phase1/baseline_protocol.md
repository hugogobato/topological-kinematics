**Work package.** WP-1.2 (fair baseline and referee protocol). **Gate.** G1.

## 1. Purpose and scope

WP-1.2 exists because the full distance matrix, the raw-data histories, and the persistence moments or signatures may subsume the proposed compact summaries. This protocol fixes the comparison before any outcome can be seen, so that a favorable baseline selection, a tuning advantage, or a leaking split cannot be chosen after the fact. It covers the offline fixed-length trajectory classification study of the pilot specification: one complete trajectory is one observation, one label per trajectory, and the primary estimand is delta, the trajectory-level macro balanced error of the compact representation minus that of the validation-selected incumbent. It also fixes the comparison rules that any later online detection study must inherit, namely trailing causal windows, identical prefix inputs for every comparator, and complete-trajectory split isolation.

The protocol does not reinterpret the scientific status of any claim. The novelty withdrawals recorded in the plan (inter-frame diagram speed, diagram trajectories in the broad form, and broad topological-velocity language) remain binding at G1, and the comparison angle remains a triangle-shape diagnostic with no direction. Section 11 states what would falsify each claim of plan Section 2.

## 2. The six comparator families and their representations

Every trajectory is represented ten ways. Six comparator families are mandatory, and the compact representation is the proposed method. All representations receive the same retained frames of the same complete trajectory, the same label, and the same split membership. The names below are exactly the names in `implementation_contract.md` Section 6 and are the keys of the feature dictionary written by the runner.

| Required family | Contract representation(s) | Construction summary | Role in the comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kramar-style consecutive speed | `speed_history` | Entire adjacent speed vector `nu_t = d(D_t, D_{t+1}) / h_t`, then the same six speed and speed-change summaries carried by the compact representation | Speed-only incumbent, mandatory. |
| Distance-matrix recurrence | `complete_distances`, `recurrence_summary` | Full strictly-upper triangle of the frame-by-frame diagram-distance matrix in row-major temporal order; plus mean and minimum distance at lags 1, 2, 4, 8 and endpoint displacement `R` | Strongest simple baseline, mandatory in both forms, so a weak recurrence summary cannot be substituted for the full matrix. |
| Raw data, where available | `raw_geometry_flat`, `raw_geometry_summary` | Family A per frame: pairwise-distance quantiles 0.1, 0.5, 0.9, the two covariance eigenvalues, and mean nearest-neighbor distance. Family B per frame: mean, standard deviation, maximum, and mean gradient energy. Flattened histories and temporal mean and standard deviation summaries. | Raw-data incumbent that requires no persistence computation. |
| Persistence moments | `moments_flat`, `moments_summary` | Per frame, `sum_i b_i^a p_i^j` for `(a, j)` in `[(0,1), (1,1), (2,1), (0,2), (1,2), (0,3)]` with `p = death - birth`; flattened histories and temporal summaries. The moments are not claimed injective. | Topological moment incumbent. |
| Moment signatures, without and with time augmentation | `moment_signature`, `moment_signature_time` | Exact level-2 signature of the piecewise-linear path through the six moment coordinates; the second adds `u` as a seventh coordinate. Dimensions 42 and 56. | Path-signature incumbent in the sense of Giusti and Lee, run as an explicit finite-vector comparator and never as a borrowed stability theorem. |
| Proposed compact diagnostics | `compact` | `L`, `R`, `eta`; the six speed and speed-change summaries; mean `cos(theta)` over valid angles; angle-valid fraction; efficiency-valid flag. | The proposed method. |

The raw-data family is present for both synthetic families because the generator interface saves raw frames for both. The "where available" clause is prospective only: if a future dataset lacks raw observations, the raw family is omitted and the omission is recorded as a deviation, never silently. The primary degree is H0; every representation is built for the primary degree and the H1 channel is reported separately with the same protocol. The W2 sensitivity branch, if run, uses the same protocol and is reported separately, with no stability constant transferred between norms.

## 3. Equal tuning, validation, and tie-breaking

Every representation is fitted, tuned, and selected under identical rules. Centering, scaling, and imputation are fit on the training split only, a missing-mask column is retained wherever a column has any training missingness, and no latent value, generator identity, or test label enters any feature. The learner grids are exactly those of `implementation_contract.md` Section 7: multinomial logistic regression with `C` in `{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10}` (4 configurations) and RBF SVM with the same `C` set and `gamma` in `{0.1/d, 1/d, 10/d}` (12 configurations), where `d` is the postprocessed feature dimension of that representation in that cell. Every (cell, representation) pair therefore receives exactly 16 hyperparameter evaluations; no representation receives an extra configuration, an extra learner family, early stopping, feature selection, or data augmentation. The numeric gamma values differ across cells by the frozen rule because `d` differs, but the grid rule and its cardinality are identical for all representations.

A cell is one `(family, sigma, stride, degree)` combination, and a separate fixed-dimensional model is fit inside each cell, as the plan Section 8 requires. Within a cell, the learner and hyperparameters are selected by validation macro balanced error on the validation split, computed with the same equal-weight cell rule used for the primary estimand (macro balanced error within each validation cell, then equal-weight average across the validation cells of that representation). Ties are broken by lower measured validation prediction seconds, then by learner name, then by the frozen grid order. The selected configuration is the one applied to the test split, and the full selection table is saved as an artifact.

Two cost quantities are frozen. First, the tie-breaking cost of a learner configuration is the total measured validation prediction seconds returned for that configuration in that cell. Second, the end-to-end cost of a representation is the sum of the pipeline stages it actually consumes: diagram extraction seconds are charged to every representation that consumes diagrams, raw geometry representations are charged frame loading and feature computation only, and every representation is charged its feature and prediction seconds. Per-trajectory stage times are recorded in the results table (`extraction_seconds`, `feature_seconds`, `prediction_seconds`, `peak_ram_bytes`), and per-cell end-to-end cost is the median over held-out trajectories. Raw per-stage costs are reported separately from the end-to-end cost, as the pilot specification requires, and diagram extraction is never hidden.

## 4. Complete-trajectory split policy and causality

The split key is the base seed. The frozen namespaces are training `1000-1039`, validation `2000-2019`, exploratory test `3000-3039`, and confirmatory test seeds starting at `10000`. Every trajectory generated from one base seed, across both families, all three classes, both noise levels, all strides, and both the primary and secondary degrees, belongs to the split of that base seed. Seed namespaces include family and class in the trajectory identifier, but split assignment is by base seed, so no family, class, noise, or resolution variant of one base seed can appear in two splits. The bootstrap clustering unit is the `(family, class, base_seed)` cluster, and all correlated variants of a cluster move together in every resample.

The unit of observation is one complete trajectory over the fixed physical horizon `[0, 1]`. No overlapping window of any trajectory is distributed across splits. The primary observation map is one frame per sample, which makes the frame windows of one trajectory inseparable by construction once their trajectory is split. For any later windowed observation map (lengths 1, 3, 5 and strides 1, 2, 4) and for any detection work, windows are trailing causal windows: the window evaluated at output time `u` contains only frames observed at or before `u`, window timestamps are end-of-window times, no window may straddle a split boundary, and every comparator receives the same prefix or trailing inputs. Window-pooled results are never pooled into the primary frame-level claim. The split manifest records one row per trajectory with at least `trajectory_id`, `base_seed`, `family`, `class`, `sigma`, `stride`, `degree`, `metric`, `split`, `cache_path`, and `diagram_hash`, and it must show that no trajectory identifier appears under more than one split.

Controls are generated under the same rule but stay outside the three-class accuracy calculation: static `z(u) = 0.5` with and without noise, the Family A rigid-translation control, the matched-speed ordering construction, and the scale-sensitivity analysis. The translation control must leave Family A diagrams unchanged within `1e-7 * max(1, filtration range)`; the matched-speed construction is a correctness and information control, never a fourth scientific class.

## 5. Incumbent selection and the eligible comparator set

After validation is complete and before any test result is opened, the full validation ranking of all ten representations is recorded by equal-weight cell-averaged validation macro balanced error. The incumbent is the best non-compact representation in that ranking. The compact representation is ranked and reported but is not eligible to be the incumbent, because delta is defined as compact minus incumbent and because the parsimony argument needs a comparator. If two representations tie exactly, the tie is broken by lower measured end-to-end validation cost, then by lower postprocessed feature dimension, then by representation name in the contract order for determinism.

The eligible comparator set is fixed from validation error alone and is frozen with the route. It contains every non-compact representation whose cell-averaged validation macro balanced error is at most 0.02 above the best non-compact validation result, that is, the incumbent's validation error. This is the conservative reading of "within 0.02 of the best validation result": the reference is the incumbent's own validation error, which yields a superset of comparators relative to using the compact error as the reference whenever compact is better on validation. Any representation in that band is eligible to defeat the parsimony route if it is cheaper than the compact representation, and a tiny raw-data or speed-summary incumbent that ties the compact method at lower cost defeats the parsimony argument by construction.

The fourfold parsimony comparison is then computed in two axes. The dimension ratio in a cell is the smallest postprocessed feature dimension among eligible comparators in that cell divided by the compact postprocessed dimension, where the postprocessed dimension is the `feature_dim` actually recorded and used to set the SVM gamma. The cost ratio in a cell is the smallest eligible end-to-end cost in that cell divided by the compact end-to-end cost, using the median held-out per-trajectory cost defined in Section 3. The reported parsimony ratio is the worst case across cells, that is, the minimum over cells of each ratio taken separately; the parsimony condition P >= 4.0 holds when the worst-case dimension ratio or the worst-case cost ratio reaches 4.0. The cell-wise worst case prevents a single favorable resolution from carrying the claim.

## 6. Decision rule, precision, and route freezing

The confirmatory evaluation uses only base seeds starting at 10000 and the frozen fitted models. Let delta be the cell-averaged macro balanced error of the compact representation minus that of the incumbent, with a paired cluster bootstrap of whole `(family, class, base_seed)` clusters, 2000 resamples, bootstrap seed 20260907, preserving the pairing of all methods and all correlated noise and resolution variants. Let `delta_95` be the paired 95 percent interval and let `delta_f` be the same quantity restricted to family `f`, evaluated with the same bootstrap within the family. The decision rule is exactly the pilot specification rule:

| Condition | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness and nuisance checks | C1 and C2 checks pass, G2 controls show no null-process events and no leakage, no missing-trajectory defect | Required for GO |
| Adequate precision | The confirmatory size was chosen in advance from `{200, 500, 1000, 2000}` base-seed clusters using the exploratory paired error variance and a target 95 percent half-width of 0.01 for the 0.02 noninferiority margin | Required for GO |
| Route (a), superiority | Upper endpoint of `delta_95` below `-0.05` | GO if the other conditions hold |
| Route (b), parsimony | Upper endpoint of `delta_95` below `0.02` and `P >= 4.0` with `P` the worst-case dimension or cost ratio of Section 5 | GO if the other conditions hold |
| Family safeguard | Upper endpoint of the Bonferroni-adjusted per-family interval `delta_f` below `0.05` for every family, so no family deterioration above 0.05 | Required for GO, on either route |
| Route freeze | The claimed route was frozen after the exploratory stage and before confirmatory seeds were opened, and is not selected afterward | Required for GO |
| Eligible set freeze | The eligible comparator set was frozen from validation before test results were opened | Required for GO |
| Precision failure | Even the largest affordable size cannot resolve the margin according to the exploratory variance projection | INDETERMINATE, planned before running |
| Realized comparison unresolved | Lower endpoint of `delta_95` is at or below `0` while no route passes | INDETERMINATE, not failure |
| Reliable domination | Lower endpoint of `delta_95` above `0`, so the incumbent is reliably better; if the incumbent is the cheaper simpler representation, this is the parsimony defeat case | INCREMENTAL-ONLY for this diagnostic task, unless the G3 memo records a separately predeclared narrower useful effect |
| Narrower useful result | A reproducible useful effect on a predeclared subgroup or alternative estimand, with a new protocol and untouched test seeds | PIVOT, assigned by the G3 memo with written reasons |
| Broken extraction, circular labels, or leakage | Any such defect found | Repair and rerun the affected stage before any decision |

No outcome of the two toy families alone establishes a universal kinematics claim or kills all possible applications. The provisional thresholds are simulation design choices, not established application utility requirements; the application requirement at WP-4.1 supersedes them.

The route is frozen by the following procedure, in this order: (1) the exploratory stage completes and its report and full validation ranking are written; (2) preprocessing, per-cell learner configurations, the incumbent, and the full ranking are frozen from training and validation only; (3) the eligible comparator set and its dimension and cost table are frozen from validation only; (4) the single confirmatory size is chosen from `{200, 500, 1000, 2000}` using the exploratory paired error variance and the 0.01 half-width target, with cost estimated before scheduling; (5) the route, superiority or parsimony, is stated in writing; it may be informed by the exploratory stage, and it is binding even if the confirmatory results would satisfy the other route; (6) a freeze record is written with timestamp, code hashes, config hash, authority hashes, chosen size, route, eligible comparator list, validation dimension and cost ratios, precision projection, and an explicit statement that confirmatory seeds have not been opened; (7) only then may confirmatory trajectories with seeds starting at 10000 be generated, once, with no peeking and no stopping when significance appears. If the freeze record is incomplete, or if the planned precision is unreachable, confirmatory evaluation is not authorized and the planned status is INDETERMINATE.

## 7. Feature schema, dimensions, and semantics

The schema below is frozen. Let `n` be the number of retained frames and `T = n - 1` the number of intervals: stride 1 gives `n = 129` and `T = 128`; stride 2 gives `n = 65` and `T = 64`; stride 4 gives `n = 33` and `T = 32`. The nominal dimension is the length of the feature vector returned by `build_representations` before imputation masks. The postprocessed dimension `d` is the nominal dimension plus one mask column for each column with any training missingness; `d` is the `feature_dim` recorded in the results table and the `d` used for the RBF SVM gamma. The ratio comparisons of Section 5 use `d`.

| Representation | Semantics | Nominal formula | Stride 1 | Stride 2 | Stride 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `compact` | `L`, `R`, `eta`; six speed and speed-change summaries; mean `cos(theta)` over valid angles; angle-valid fraction; efficiency-valid flag | constant | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| `speed_history` | adjacent speeds `nu_t`, then the same six summaries | `T + 6` | 134 | 70 | 38 |
| `complete_distances` | strictly-upper triangle of the `n` by `n` distance matrix, row-major temporal order | `n (n - 1) / 2` | 8256 | 2080 | 528 |
| `recurrence_summary` | mean and minimum distance at lags 1, 2, 4, 8, plus `R` | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| `raw_geometry_flat` | Family A per frame: quantiles 0.1, 0.5, 0.9, two covariance eigenvalues, mean nearest-neighbor distance; Family B per frame: mean, standard deviation, maximum, mean gradient energy; history concatenated | A `6 n`, B `4 n` | 774 / 516 | 390 / 260 | 198 / 132 |
| `raw_geometry_summary` | temporal mean and standard deviation of each per-frame raw quantity | A 12, B 8 | 12 / 8 | 12 / 8 | 12 / 8 |
| `moments_flat` | per-frame moment coordinates `[(0,1), (1,1), (2,1), (0,2), (1,2), (0,3)]`, history concatenated | `6 n` | 774 | 390 | 198 |
| `moments_summary` | temporal mean and standard deviation of each of the six moment coordinates | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| `moment_signature` | exact level-2 signature of the piecewise-linear path in the six moment coordinates | `6 + 6^2` | 42 | 42 | 42 |
| `moment_signature_time` | the same path with `u` appended as a seventh coordinate | `7 + 7^2` | 56 | 56 | 56 |

The six speed and speed-change summaries are frozen as follows: mean, standard deviation, and maximum of the adjacent speed vector; mean signed speed-change; mean absolute speed-change; and maximum absolute speed-change. The pilot specification says "six" while enumerating five, and the inherited ambiguity is resolved by adding the mean signed speed-change to the five named quantities, exactly as the implementation contract records. The consequence is that the compact representation has 12 nominal features, not 11; the parenthetical "(11 features)" in `implementation_contract.md` Section 6 is a stale count that this freeze supersedes, and the correction is submitted for the execution log. The standard deviation is the numpy default population standard deviation (`ddof = 0`), matching the training standardization convention.

The semantics of the remaining compact entries are fixed by the metric interface: `L` is the sum of adjacent distances, `R` is the endpoint distance, `eta = R / L` is defined only when `L > 0`, speed is timestamped at interval midpoints, speed-change uses the midpoint denominator, `theta` is the interior angle of the metric triangle and lies in `[0, pi]`, and the efficiency-valid flag is `1` when `L > 2 (T - 1) e` and `0` otherwise, with `e` the cell calibration of Section 9. Straight continuation gives `theta = pi` and exact reversal gives `theta = 0`; both are valid and are reported. The turn companion `tau = pi - theta` is reported with `theta`, never alone, and no direction is defined.

For the signature representations, the six moment coordinates entering the path are scaled with training-only per-coordinate mean and standard deviation, and the time coordinate `u` is used unscaled on `[0, 1]`. The unscaled time channel is a WP-1.2 freeze: the horizon is fixed by design and identical for every trajectory and split, so scaling time would only add a training-dependent reparameterization, while the physics-facing interpretation of the time channel stays in physical units. This choice is part of the frozen schema and must be verified against `signatures.py` and the model pipeline at smoke; any divergence is a deviation, not a silent change. The exact level-2 signature convention is Chen's identity as fixed in `implementation_contract.md`, with analytic unit tests on a straight path and an L-shape.

## 8. Missing and degenerate value policies

The following list is the complete missing and degenerate value policy set for the comparison. Every case is either defined, explicitly undefined with a flag, or excluded with a recorded count; nothing is silently imputed, deleted, or repaired. Trajectories with a missing window or an extraction failure are excluded from the primary offline analysis with recorded counts, never interpolated and never repaired by pooling, and a nonzero exclusion count is treated as a defect to repair before any GO rather than as an analysis choice.

| ID | Situation | Policy | Reported with |
|---|---|---|---|
| MV-01 | Missing window or extraction failure | whole trajectory excluded from the primary analysis; count recorded; no interpolation, no pooling | exclusion table; `status` column |
| MV-02 | Any feature column with training missingness | NaN at construction; training-only median imputation; one retained missing-mask column per affected feature column; constant columns map to zero; no test statistics | mask columns; `feature_dim` |
| MV-03 | `eta` when `L = 0` | undefined (NaN internally, null in JSON); flag recorded; no imputation | `L`; flag; mask |
| MV-04 | `theta` when `a = 0` or `b = 0` | undefined; step counted as unresolved; contributes to no angle statistic | angle-valid fraction |
| MV-05 | mean `cos(theta)` with no valid angles | NaN; imputed with mask at model fit | angle-valid fraction; mask |
| MV-06 | speed-change summaries with fewer than two speed-change values | declared NaN; impossible at the frozen grids but policy stated | mask if it ever occurs |
| MV-07 | efficiency floor `L <= 2 (T - 1) e` | efficiency-valid flag set to 0; trajectory retained; no trajectory deletion | flag; coverage |
| MV-08 | angle floor `min(a, b) <= 2 e` | step resolution flagged; unresolved steps excluded from angle statistics only | valid fraction; coverage |
| MV-09 | calibration population absent for a cell | the floor is undefined for that cell; the floor is disabled with an annotated flag; confirmatory evaluation is blocked until the training-split static-noise population exists | calibration table |
| MV-10 | cosine argument outside `[-1, 1]` | clip to `[-1, 1]`; cosine-anomaly flag when `abs(z) > 1 + 1e-12`; never hidden | anomaly flag |
| MV-11 | essential classes with infinite death | removed before any metric call; per-window count recorded | essential counts |
| MV-12 | empty finite diagram | valid; moment coordinates zero; no missing value | moment features |
| MV-13 | nonpositive interval `h_t` | input error; rejected; no repair | status |
| MV-14 | `L = 0`, zero-step, or floor cases | flagged or abstained, never dropped | flags; coverage |
| MV-15 | `valid_fraction` | angle-valid fraction for `compact`; `1.0` for every other representation | results table |
| MV-16 | straight and reversal angle cases | valid; reported; never deleted | angle tables |

## 9. Abstention and coverage reporting

Abstention is a reported flag, never a deletion. Calibration is per cell defined by `(family, degree, resolution, metric)`: the population is the adjacent diagram distances computed on the training-split static-noise trajectories at `sigma = 0.05` for that cell, `e` is the 95th percentile with numpy's default linear interpolation, and calibrations are never pooled across families, degrees, resolutions, or metrics. At `sigma = 0` the calibration is exactly zero and only exact-zero abstention applies, where exact zero is the value returned by the declared numerical-zero convention. The efficiency floor is `L <= 2 (T - 1) e` and the angle floor is `min(a, b) <= 2 e`.

Performance is reported both with and without the floors for every regime, together with validity fractions and coverage. Coverage is reported per cell and per family as the fraction of steps that supplied a valid angle and the fraction of trajectories whose efficiency flag is valid, alongside macro balanced error, per-class confusion matrices, and the family-specific error differences. Straight and reversal cases remain reported. No trajectory is selectively deleted to improve a summary, and no test trajectory is discarded after outcomes are seen.

## 10. Family safeguard and multiplicity

The family safeguard is simultaneous and Bonferroni-adjusted over the two raw families. Each family-specific paired interval for `delta_f` is computed at `1 - 0.05 / 2 = 0.975` coverage, so the two family statements hold simultaneously with at most 5 percent family-wise error, and GO on either route requires the upper endpoint below `0.05` in both families. One family may not be traded away for a pooled result. The pooled `delta_95` interval remains at 95 percent and is used for the route thresholds; the family intervals are additional required conditions, not substitutes. If a family-specific upper endpoint exceeds `0.05`, the GO is blocked, the result is reported as a family-specific failure, and the G3 memo records whether the outcome is INCREMENTAL-ONLY for this task or PIVOT to a narrower claim.

## 12. Verification, artifacts, and recorded readings

WP-1.2 passes only if an independent rerun obtains the same feature dimensions and no trajectory appears in multiple splits, and only if no comparator is disadvantaged by timing or tuning. The verification checklist is: V1, the smoke manifest reproduces the nominal and postprocessed dimensions of Section 7 in every cell; V2, the split manifest has no trajectory in more than one split and keeps all variants of a base seed together; V3, the learner evaluation count is exactly 16 per (cell, representation) for every representation; V4, preprocessing statistics are training-only and hashable; V5, the decision arithmetic is reproducible from the frozen artifacts with the frozen bootstrap seed and 2000 resamples; V6, the prohibited-language audit is clean for every claim status of Section 11; V7, the cost accounting is reproducible from the results table columns. Failure of V1 through V5 resets the affected empirical results; failure of V6 blocks the G1 packet.

The artifacts of this work package are this protocol, `research_review/results/phase1/claims_to_tests.yaml`, the smoke outputs under `research_review/results/phase1/smoke/`, the split manifest produced by the runner under `research_review/results/g3/splits/`, the exploratory outputs under `research_review/results/g3/exploratory/`, the route freeze record at `research_review/results/g3/route_freeze.md`, the confirmatory outputs under `research_review/results/g3/confirmatory/`, and the G1 decision at `research_review/results/phase1/decision_packet_G1.md`. Results are never gitignored.
