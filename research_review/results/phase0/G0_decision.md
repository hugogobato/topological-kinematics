# G0 decision record: Phase 0 definitions, estimands, and correctness

**Decision.** G0 is PASS for the restricted finite-metric operational model. The project is authorized to continue to the Phase 1 prior-art and baseline work packages (WP-1.1, WP-1.2). G0 authorizes nothing beyond that.

**Scope of the decision.** A finite sequence of finite persistence diagrams with finite birth and death coordinates, a fixed diagram metric (bottleneck with L-infinity ground norm), and strictly increasing timestamps defines a nonempty, finite-distance model whose discrete path diagnostics are total or explicitly undefined with a stated policy. The decision covers the metric-path quantities and their implementation only.

**What G0 does not cover.** Data-extraction and raw-pipeline correctness (G2), the central empirical hypothesis and simulation study (G3), applications (G4), and theory (G5). It also does not establish novelty, predictive value, causal identification, domain independence, or any application impact. The prior-art statuses recorded in the plan remain binding: inter-frame persistence-diagram speed and diagram trajectories are established background, and the comparison angle is a triangle-shape diagnostic only.
